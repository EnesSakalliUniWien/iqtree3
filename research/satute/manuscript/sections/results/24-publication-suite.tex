\subsection*{Publication-scale nucleotide power curves}

We next tested whether the additional spectral directions improved detection under models that distinguish among them. Fixed GTR+F, GTR+F+G4 and GTR+F+I+G4 models were evaluated in 1,000 paired replicates per cell, with 100, 250 or 1,000 sites; focal lengths 4--12; and five-taxon external or 16-taxon internal targets. The merged grid contains 864,000 detail rows and 2,016 summaries, with complete replicate identifiers and no JC-collapse mismatch.

Both GTR+F+G4 statistics lost power as the focal branch length increased, and longer alignments shifted the transition rightward (Fig.~\ref{fig-gtr-g4-power}). Eigenvalue weighting produced the largest shift at 100 sites, where published SatuTe left the high-power regime while the eigenvalue-weighted SatuTe statistic still detected coherence. Supplementary Figs. S3--S5 provide paired differences and the corresponding GTR+F and GTR+F+I+G4 curves.

The largest GTR+F+G4 increase was 0.241 unadjusted (95\% bootstrap interval, 0.215--0.269), 0.414 under Benjamini--Yekutieli (BY; 0.384--0.446) and 0.532 under taxon-pair Bonferroni in the inferred-ML-tree analysis (0.501--0.562). All occurred at 100 sites on the 16-taxon target. Exact McNemar \(P\) values were \(5.7\times10^{-73}\), \(4.7\times10^{-125}\) and \(1.4\times10^{-160}\), respectively. Taxon-pair Bonferroni is not defined for either fixed-topology analysis and was therefore not applied to those scenarios.

\subsection*{Model misspecification changes absolute operating characteristics}

We next simulated GTR+F but evaluated the same alignments under GTR, JC, K2P or F81. Across 960,000 detail rows, JC and K2P were more liberal than correctly specified GTR, whereas F81, which retains base composition, was substantially closer (Fig.~\ref{fig-gtr-misspec-absolute}). Thus a small paired formula difference under a misspecified model does not imply correct absolute calibration.

\begin{figure}[p]
  \centering
  \includegraphics[width=\textwidth,height=0.82\textheight,keepaspectratio]{figures/figure_model_misspecification.pdf}
  \caption{\textbf{Model misspecification changes absolute rejection rates.} GTR+F simulations evaluated with JC (\textbf{a,b}), K2P (\textbf{c,d}) or F81 (\textbf{e,f}) on the five-taxon external target (\textbf{a,c,e}) and 16-taxon internal target (\textbf{b,d,f}). Each panel overlays the correctly specified GTR+F true-tree reference, misspecified true-tree and true-topology/ML-length analyses using unadjusted tests, and the misspecified inferred-ML-tree analysis using taxon-pair Bonferroni. Line style denotes alignment length. JC and K2P produce high spurious rejection at long branches, and this distortion increases rather than disappears with alignment length; F81 is much closer to the correctly specified reference. The gray band marks 0--5\%.}
  \makeatletter\cref@old@label{fig-gtr-misspec-absolute}\makeatother
\end{figure}

Within misspecified analyses, the largest weighted gains were 0.131 unadjusted, 0.206 under taxon Bonferroni and 0.209 under BY. JC remained an exact formula-control because its non-stationary eigenvalues are equal. Supplementary Figs. S10--S12 show JC, K2P and F81 paired differences and weighted curves.

\subsection*{Matched protein power varies strongly among models}

The matched protein suite used fixed LG+G4, WAG+G4, JTT+G4 and Q.pfam+G4 models. Eigenvalue-weighted-minus-published-SatuTe differences were never negative among the applicable scenario--rule cells. In the inferred-ML-tree analysis, maximum taxon-pair-Bonferroni gains were 0.268 for LG, 0.410 for WAG, 0.546 for JTT and 0.304 for Q.pfam. The consolidated inferred-tree comparison shows the exact JC control beside the GTR+F+G4 and LG+G4 separation (Fig.~\ref{fig-cross-model-power}); complete published-SatuTe, eigenvalue-weighted-SatuTe and paired-difference curves show the matrix-specific transitions (Supplementary Figs. S6--S9).

\subsection*{Independent truth-labelled calibration}

Independent calibration used 5,000 exact-null replicates per cell, split equally between threshold training and held-out evaluation, plus 2,000 50:50 mixed-null replicates. In the GTR mixed-null design, power ranged from 0.929 to 0.989 and empirical FDR from zero to 0.00386. Formula-paired differences were at most 0.000571 for power and 0.000143 for FDR, confirming that the larger publication-grid effects are localized to the saturation boundary (Fig.~\ref{fig-fdr-calibration}).
