\subsection*{JC reconstruction recovers the original SatuTe behavior}

The exact JC collapse provides a no-change control for the extension. We therefore retained the original SatuTe simulation design: five- and 16-taxon trees, 100--10,000 sites, focal lengths 0.1--10 and 1,000 JC replicates per point. Four analyses separate branch-length estimation from topology selection: true tree and lengths; true tree with maximum-likelihood (ML) lengths; an ML tree and lengths; and the same inferred-tree analysis with taxon-pair Bonferroni correction.

With the true tree and lengths, the fraction of phylogenetically informative alignments fell from near one to the 5\% null level as the focal branch lengthened; longer alignments retained signal further (Fig.~\ref{fig-jc-baseline}). ML length estimation on the true topology closely followed this reference. Selecting and testing a topology on the same alignment was liberal at long branches, reaching approximately 15\% for the external and 50\% for the internal target. Taxon-pair Bonferroni correction largely restored the reference curve, but became conservative for subtrees with nearly indistinguishable sequences.

For 100-site 16-taxon alignments, correct topology recovery fell from 63.2\% at focal length 0.1 to 4.5\% at length 1 and nearly zero beyond length 3. Yet bootstrap support for the split reached 100\% by length 0.8 and stayed there while Bonferroni SatuTe calls fell to the 3.5--4.6\% null range (Supplementary Tables S2 and S3). This establishes that high split support does not imply recoverable history across the split.

JC is the prespecified formula control: its three non-stationary directions have equal eigenvalues, so weighted and published SatuTe curves coincide exactly. Models with distinct eigenvalues separate chiefly for short alignments at the long-branch detection boundary.

\subsection*{Decision changes are concentrated on the focal branch}

Before extending the comparison beyond JC, we tested whether weighting created a nonspecific tree-wide score shift. At branch length 12 and 100 sites, focal-branch increases were 14.5--22.0 percentage points under GTR+G4 and 38.1--39.1 points under LG+G4, whereas adjacent branches changed by essentially zero (Supplementary Fig. S1). Thus the added signal was localized to the deliberately lengthened branch.
