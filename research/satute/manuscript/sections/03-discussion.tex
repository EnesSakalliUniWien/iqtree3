\section*{Discussion}

This study establishes a continuous extension of SatuTe rather than a replacement test. Persistence weighting leaves the JC statistic exactly unchanged, adds power only when a fitted model contains distinguishable non-stationary directions, and preserves error control in independent truth-labelled experiments. Correct treatment of invariant sites separately restores the null that a zero-rate category otherwise violates. Across these settings, the extension improved sensitivity at the long-branch detection boundary without changing the branch, null hypothesis or one-sided decision that define SatuTe.

The historical JC reconstruction clarifies what this decision measures. SatuTe asks whether an alignment retains detectable dependence between the subtrees separated by a branch. Bootstrap and likelihood-ratio support instead quantify reproducibility or preference for a split \cite{felsenstein1985confidence,hoang2017,lemoine2018}. In the 16-taxon control, bootstrap support remained complete after topology recovery collapsed and SatuTe approached its null rejection rate. A non-significant SatuTe result therefore identifies insufficient branch-specific signal in the tested alignment and model; it does not deny common ancestry.

The gain was deliberately narrow rather than universal. It appeared chiefly for short alignments crossing the long-branch detection boundary and was negligible when both formulas had high power or both had reached the null floor. Branch-neighbourhood audits showed that weighting did not indiscriminately raise scores across a tree. Its magnitude depended strongly on the substitution matrix: JTT produced broader shifts than LG or Q.pfam, demonstrating that alphabet size alone does not determine the recoverable spectral information.

Model adequacy remains at least as consequential as the choice of statistic. JC and K2P analyses of GTR data were too liberal, whereas F81 was closer to the correctly specified result. The JC zero-difference control establishes formula equality, not robustness of JC to misspecification. Likewise, invariant sites create a distinct structural problem: a zero-rate category remains ancestrally dependent across an infinite focal branch, so placing it in a product-form null introduces a positive mean that studentization amplifies with alignment length. Including its joint probability only in the null denominator restored exact centering and standard-normal calibration. The exploratory \(+I+R4\) analysis is a stress test; fixed GTR+F+I+G4 provides the publication benchmark.

The empirical examples show why a local diagnostic is useful after these statistical controls are satisfied. Ribosomal proteins retained signal across nearly all windows, whereas 16S rRNA contained broad saturated regions and lost signal for the Eukaryota branch in its fastest category. In HCV, gene-level tests were uniformly informative, yet short-window losses concentrated at synonymous-enriched third positions. An alignment-wide pass can therefore mask within-locus saturation. Branch- and window-specific curves can instead direct model checks, partitioning or selective masking without discarding an otherwise informative locus. Weighting reduced threshold crossings without erasing either biological contrast.

The interpretation remains bounded. These reference-panel analyses localize signal; they neither select the true topology nor estimate viral population diversity. The HCV result is additionally limited by ten genomes, overlapping short windows and model refitting at that scale. Significance also does not guarantee correct topology or model adequacy. Non-stationary or non-reversible extensions would make the root identifiable and require a root-aware null.

Within the present reversible framework, the evidence supports a clear workflow. Published SatuTe remains the reference statistic and exact JC control; the weighted result reports sensitivity gained from spectral directions that persist under the fitted model; the corrected mixture null is required when invariant sites are explicit. Both statistics should be reported with paired differences, multiplicity correction, conventional branch support and model-adequacy checks. Together, these views connect the theoretical extension, controlled operating characteristics and biological examples: they make loss of historical signal testable where phylogenetic conclusions are actually made, at individual branches and within specific alignment regions.
