\section*{Results}

We evaluated the extension in a prespecified sequence. We first derived the persistence-weighted statistic and its invariant-mixture null, then verified exact agreement with published SatuTe under JC and reconstructed the original simulation behavior. We next tested null centering, paired power and model dependence under nucleotide and protein models, followed by independent truth-labelled error-control experiments. Finally, we asked whether the resulting branch- and region-specific diagnoses remained interpretable in Tree-of-Life and viral alignments.
