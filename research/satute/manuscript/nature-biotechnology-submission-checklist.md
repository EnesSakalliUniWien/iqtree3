## Author-dependent items required before submission

- [ ] Confirm the complete author list, author order and consent from every author.
- [ ] Add each author’s institutional affiliation and identify the corresponding author with email, postal address and telephone number.
- [ ] Replace the funding placeholder with all funders and grant numbers, or state explicitly that no specific funding was received.
- [ ] Confirm the competing-interests declaration for every author.
- [ ] Decide whether to use double-blind review; if so, produce anonymized manuscript and supplement files.
- [ ] Complete the Nature Portfolio Reporting Summary for life-sciences research.
- [ ] Deposit the code and source data in a versioned public archive and replace the prospective DOI statement with the final DOI.
- [ ] Confirm whether closely related manuscripts, preprints or prior discussions with an editor must be disclosed in the cover letter.
- [ ] Confirm permission and licensing for reused source datasets; no artwork from the earlier paper is reused in this draft.
- [ ] Supply individual source-data files for every main figure if requested during revision.

## Final production checks

- [ ] Check all figure labels at final publication dimensions (maximum width 180 mm; target 5–7 pt sans-serif labels).
- [ ] Confirm at least 300 dpi for raster components and retain vector PDF wherever possible.
- [ ] Put each Supplementary figure and its legend on a single page if requested at accepted-manuscript production.
- [ ] Ensure every statistical legend identifies the center, error interval, sample size, test and exact *P* value where applicable.
- [ ] Re-run `./satute manuscript`, `./satute check audit` and `./satute check figures` after metadata and archive DOI are final.
